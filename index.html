The Supervisor's Field Kit

Free companion sheets for readers of Run the Shift

Three sheets you can print and use on the floor. They are built around the numbers in the book — the UPH scale, the dock-to-stock window, the corrective-action clocks — so they will make more sense with the book open beside them. Print them on Letter unless your office has a large-format printer.

Every shift

Shift Handover Sheet

What the next lead actually needs from you: open order queues, open dock receipts, equipment locked out and who holds the lock, open safety items on their clocks, and a head count of who is still in the building.

Letter · 8.5×11″ Tabloid · 11×17″

Every week

Weekly Floor Planner

Seven days across three bands — shift, safety, quality — with the open-items list beside it, and the Perfect Order Rate calculation along the bottom so you can see what four decent sub-rates actually multiply out to.

Letter · 11×8.5″ Tabloid · 17×11″

Once — lasts a year

52 Toolbox Talk Topics

One topic per week of the year, each tagged with the chapter that covers it. For the Thursday morning when you need something worth ten minutes and you have not had time to think about it.

Letter · 8.5×11″ Tabloid · 11×17″

These are personal working sheets, not facility records. They do not replace your site's own forms, logs, or safety program, and nothing here is legal or safety-engineering advice. Confirm anything you intend to write into a procedure with your own EHS professional. In an emergency, call 911.
